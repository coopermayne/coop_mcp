{# The detail reader — ONE modal serving every "there's more behind this" target on a page. On /food that's an item (its own nutrients, which the summed rings can't show), a nutrient ring (its target, which an arc can only imply), and the circled "i" (the day's notes); on /journal it's the day's food icon (what was eaten). Each target carries its whole payload in `data-detail` as JSON — {kicker, title, rows, note, empty} — so opening one is display-only, with no fetch and nothing to look up. A row is {label, value}; `value` may be empty, which is how the journal's plain list of dishes renders without a figure column. Included by both pages rather than copied into each: one delegated handler on document means a page adds a target just by rendering `data-detail` on it. #}

{# Rows are label-left / figure-right with a hairline between, so a breakdown reads as a small table without drawing one. #}
